About Adlai Hospital Kasoa

Your Trusted Healthcare Partner in Kasoa

At Adlai Community Hospital in Kasoa (Central Region), situated conveniently at the Nurses Quarters opposite Benab Filling Station, we provide professional medical attention, emergency support, and family-centered care. Our specialists ensure reliable diagnostics and effective treatments.

Our Medical Services

Comprehensive Healthcare Services

Out Patient Services (General OPD)

Expert diagnostic evaluations, primary care, and outpatient medical consultations.

Emergency Services

24/7 prompt trauma, urgent care, and critical medical intervention support.

In-Patient Services

Comfortable admission wards managed round-the-clock by professional nurses and doctors.

Maternity Services

Safe delivery, prenatal care, and comprehensive maternal health support.

Ante Natal Care (ANC)

Routine check-ups, monitoring, and educational health sessions for expectant mothers.

Post Natal Care (PNC)

Post-delivery recovery evaluation, newborn check-ups, and lactation counseling.

Laboratory

Accurate, rapid blood tests, urinalysis, and comprehensive diagnostic screenings.

Ultra Sound Scan

Advanced imaging solutions for accurate obstetric, gynecological, and internal diagnostics.

Wound Care & Suturing

Professional cleaning, dressing, minor surgical procedures, and laceration suturing.

Hypertension & Diabetes Clinic

Specialized chronic condition monitoring, routine check-ups, and expert management.
