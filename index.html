About Adlai Hospital

Dedicated To Your Family’s Health & Well-Being

At Adlai Community Hospital, we believe that quality healthcare should be compassionate, accessible, and centered around the family. Our team of dedicated medical doctors, nurses, and laboratory specialists are committed to giving you the highest standard of preventive and clinical care.
